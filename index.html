Parents Ask

COMMON
QUESTIONS

Do you work with cheerleaders and dancers?
Yes. Flip Lab focuses heavily on tumbling for cheer and dance athletes, especially confidence and clean technique.
Can my athlete train with a friend?
Yes. Shared and small group lessons are available when athletes are similar in age and skill level.
What if my athlete has a mental block?
Coach Steve works with athletes physically and mentally, using progressions that rebuild trust and confidence.
Are Saturdays available?
Saturdays are unavailable. Communication is generally available 8 AM–5 PM.
What if we're running late?
Please arrive on time. Lessons run back to back, so late arrivals may mean a shorter lesson.
What if we need to cancel?
Text Coach Steve at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged.
